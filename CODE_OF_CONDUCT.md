# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

BitBySoft is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in our projects and community.

We encourage collaboration, constructive feedback, and professional communication.

## Expected Behaviour

* Be respectful and considerate.
* Provide constructive feedback.
* Welcome different perspectives.
* Focus on improving the project.
* Communicate professionally.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks.
* Offensive or abusive language.
* Spam or disruptive behaviour.
* Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors whose behaviour violates this Code of Conduct.
